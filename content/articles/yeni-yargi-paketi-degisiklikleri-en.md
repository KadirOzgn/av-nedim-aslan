The new Judicial Package, which was passed by the Grand National Assembly of Turkey and became law, has introduced very important changes in the criminal justice system and the execution regime. These reforms aim to both accelerate the trial processes and reorganize the execution conditions in some crime types.

#### Changes in the Execution Regime and Probation

One of the most important regulations that stand out in the new package is the updates on probation periods and conditional release rates. The execution rates in some crimes have been changed, taking steps that will have a direct effect on the duration of prisoners' stay in penitentiary institutions. In addition, time limits and control mechanisms regarding judicial control decisions have been tied to stricter rules.

#### Updates in Criminal Procedure

Within the scope of the Code of Criminal Procedure (CMK), regulations have been made to ensure uniformity in detention periods at the investigation stage, search and seizure procedures in IT crimes, and regional appeal (istinaf) and cassation appeal (temyiz) application periods.

* **Legal Remedy Application Periods:** With the regulation made, regional appeal and cassation appeal application periods have been made uniform as **two weeks** starting from the notification. By this means, it is aimed to prevent loss of rights due to missing deadlines.
* **Judicial Control Limits:** The process of periodically reviewing the status of suspects under judicial control decisions and the lifting of judicial control has been facilitated.

These comprehensive legislative changes directly affect ongoing cases and convicts at the execution stage.
