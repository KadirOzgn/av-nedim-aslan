Child custody is one of the most contested and sensitive issues in divorce cases. In Turkish Law, regulations regarding custody concern public order, and the judge is not bound by the demands of the parties when making a decision. Here, the single and most important criterion is the principle of **\"Best Interests of the Child\"**.

#### Fundamental Elements Determining Custody

While determining the best interests of the child, the child's physical, mental, moral, and social development is taken into account. The main factors the judge considers in this assessment are:

* **Age of the Child:** Except in exceptional cases, the custody of children in the age group in need of maternal care and affection is granted to the mother.
* **Education and Social Environment:** If the child is already attending a school or has an accustomed social/physical environment, maintaining this environment is preferred.
* **Condition of the Parents:** The lifestyle of the parents, income levels, housing and educational opportunities they can offer to the child are meticulously examined. However, financial insufficiency alone does not prevent the acquisition of custody.

#### Hearing the Child's Opinion (Age of Discretion)

Children who have reached a certain maturity (usually age 8 and above) are also asked for their own opinions in the presence of the court or accompanied by a pedagogue. In accordance with the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child and our domestic law, the preference of the child of age to express themselves plays a very decisive role in the judge's decision.

#### Personal Relationship Establishment and Joint Custody

A personal relationship is established by the judge between the child and the parent to whom custody is not granted. This relationship is a legal obligation to prevent the severance of parental bonds and ensure the child has a healthy psychology. In recent years, the **\"Joint Custody\"** institution, which entered our law through Court of Cassation decisions, can also be applied if the parties agree and it is in the interest of the child.
